july went fast. most of it was meeting new people and catching up with old friends. for a while i'd thought my life wasn't exciting enough to share, so this is me sharing it anyway.

i went backpacking for the first time in years, with friends in the hoover wilderness. i carried a full pack on a trail again. at night we went stargazing.

there were two weekend trips. one was to sea ranch, california. the other was a weekend in new york city. somewhere in there i also went horseback riding on the beach.

back home, the weekends were bbq and poker with friends.

music has been the fuel for a lot of my optimism, and this month it was all electronic: house, techno, afro, progressive house. the track at the top of this post is where you are by john summit.
